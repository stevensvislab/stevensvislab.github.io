## What you should expect

### What we work on

Our mission is to help people make sense of complex data. We combine methods from AI, data science, and human-computer interaction to develop interactive systems for data visualization and analysis.

- **[Visualizations for Traceable and Defensible Human-AI Collaboration](/#human-ai-collaboration):** goal tracking · bi-directional communication · interactive reasoning · uncertainty
- **[Making AI Trustworthy in Education Through Interactive Visualization](/#ai-in-education):** measuring trustworthiness · responsive pedagogy · personal data tracking · self-reflection
- **[Visual Analytics for Expert-Driven Scientific Discovery](/#scientific-discovery):** multidimensional visualization · spatial and 3D data · automated hypothesis testing
- **[Bias-Aware Visual Analytics Using Interaction Traces](/#bias-aware-va):** interaction traces · analytic provenance · cognitive bias · mixed-initiative guidance

Browse [all of our papers](/papers/) to see the kinds of systems, studies, and guidelines we produce.

### Who we're looking for

We're looking for self-motivated students who want to build visual analytics, human-centered AI, and data science tools to solve pressing human-centered, data-driven problems. Nobody arrives knowing everything; these are the skills we use and teach along the way:

- **Data visualization:** temporal, networks, tabular, text, geospatial, 3D
- **Data mining and analysis:** regression, clustering, dimensionality reduction, aligning with domain needs, reporting, dashboards
- **AI and machine learning:** training, fine-tuning, evaluating, prompt engineering
- **Human subjects research:** think-aloud, interviewing, mixed methods, thematic analysis
- **UI/UX:** design, interaction, prototyping, implementation, evaluation

In your application, tell us what you're curious about and why. Be yourself rather than writing what you think we want to hear.

### How we do research: a typical expedition

1. **Meet a domain expert with a data problem.** Someone is sitting down to look at data and make a decision, and the tools they have aren't enough.
2. **Map the terrain.** Building tools requires three things: the *task* (what do we need to make sense of?), the *data* (where will answers come from?), and the *users* (who is interpreting the results?).
3. **Build the tool.** Systems, interfaces, algorithms, and libraries for doing data science, for experts and novices alike, co-designed with the people who will use them.
4. **Study it in the field.** We study how people use our tools to interpret data and how interaction and visualization impact decision-making, through mixed methods, human-subjects studies with domain experts, and longitudinal deployments.
5. **Share what we learned.** Open-source software, empirical studies, and design guidelines for the community.

## Resources for the journey

### Why Stevens?

Stevens Institute of Technology sits on Castle Point in Hoboken, New Jersey, directly across the Hudson River from Manhattan. Students are a short trip from New York City's technology, finance, media, and healthcare communities.

### Funding

Ph.D. students in the [Ph.D. in Computer Science](https://www.stevens.edu/program/computer-science-doctoral-program) and [Ph.D. in Data Science](https://www.stevens.edu/program/data-science-phd) programs receive fully funded doctoral study. For Stevens master's and undergraduate students, research credit, independent studies, and paid positions may be available depending on the term.

### Partner labs

On campus, we work alongside:

- **[Hanlon Financial Systems Center](https://www.stevens.edu/school-business/hanlon-financial-systems-center):** Industry-grade financial technology labs that replicate the tools used at finance companies.
- **[Stevens XR Lab](https://www.stevens.edu/it/services/xrlab):** Extended-reality spaces and equipment for immersive visualization and interaction.

### Internships

Summer research internships in industry and at national labs are a great way to grow as a researcher. Adam interned at NASA JPL, Adobe Research, and MIT Lincoln Laboratory during his Ph.D. and is happy to talk through internship options with lab members.

## How to apply

If you made it this far and liked what you saw, then give us a shout :)

### Prospective Ph.D. students

Apply through one of these Stevens programs and name Adam Coscia as a potential advisor in your statement:

- **[Ph.D. in Computer Science](https://www.stevens.edu/program/computer-science-doctoral-program):** Fully funded doctoral study in AI/ML, algorithms, and human-centered computing.
- **[Ph.D. in Data Science](https://www.stevens.edu/program/data-science-phd):** Fully funded doctoral study in AI/ML, big data, and statistical methods.

### Current Stevens master's and undergraduate students

You don't need prior research experience. If a project here sparks your curiosity, email Adam with a few sentences on what interests you, your CV, and a link to anything you've built (a class project counts!). Research credit, independent studies, and paid positions may be available depending on the term.

- **[M.S. in Human-Centered AI](https://www.stevens.edu/program/masters-in-human-centered-ai):** Training to design user-focused AI systems, and a natural path into lab projects.

### Visiting research scholars and interns

Visiting students, scholars, and summer interns are welcome to join the expedition too. Email Adam with a few sentences on what you'd like to work on, your CV, and when and for how long you'd like to visit.
